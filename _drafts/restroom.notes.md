History and Context
* NYC is 93/100 on availability of public restrooms to people (100 major cities in the US) https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toilets_in_New_York_City
* Nicest bathroom: Bryant Park (https://www.nytimes.com/2017/04/05/nyregion/bryant-park-restroom-renovation.html)
 * $280k to renovate
 * $271k/yr to upkeep

Finances
	* Costs $3.6M to build a bathroom (https://nycpolitics101.substack.com/p/cost-of-maintaining-public-toilets-in-nyc)
	* Seems really high
 * Portaland Loo estimate at $1M per unit
 * Over 887k to install a prefab toilet, primarily due to self-imposed regulation
 * https://nycpolitics101.substack.com/p/why-nyc-public-toilets-cost-so-much
 * Estimate of 117k per year to maintain a bathroom 
 * In the 90’s there was an initiative to install bathrooms at 0 cost, but was nixed because it relied on advertising (comparison to NYC WiFi initiative which actually generates revenue for the city)

Intro to park inspection program data
* Great program, great website with data here: https://www.nycgovparks.org/park-features/parks-inspection-program
* Does bathroom inspections as part of its scope - does not cover all public bathrooms, but is a good proxy for it
* Started in 1997, most restrooms get 2 inspections per year, some more, some less (link to distribution graph)

Facts
* Cleanliness trends to more clean, covid 19 bump
* Men's rooms are slightly messier than women's rooms
* Overall issues in bathrooms dominated by trash and there not being enough soap (where the soap at?!)
* Geographic trends for overall cleanliness and then other areas
    * General distribution looks good, some uneven'ness but if you remove the parks it looks fairly well distributed
    * Bathroom quality issues have some clusters - williamsbury, far out in queens, bay ridge
* Correlation between quality and measures like median income and population are marginal - directional with what you might expect, but not strong
    * Surprising but "good" result - money does not mean cleaner, this indicates an equitable distribution of cleanliness in the city
